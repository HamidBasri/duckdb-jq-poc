The whole idea in one picture

Data comes in as tables and goes out as JSON documents. Every value on the way is moved by a mapping. Sometimes a value doesn't fit, and then something specific happens to the trade. Two engines, DuckDB and jq, do all of this; the guide ends by comparing them.

Our example trade

Our trade · TR0000000042

An amendment to an earlier trade: buy 1,250 of an option at 101.5 US dollars, with a bank called Aurora Bank. Its row has some typical mess in it: a side written Buy , a quantity written 1,250, a day-first date, and no settlement date.

Every page shows what happens to this one trade at that step. By the end you will have seen it go from a messy row to a finished JSON document, and seen how each engine gets it there.
